Vault-wide authoring rules:

- Sentence-case headings; lowercase-hyphen filenames.
- Every note gets `title` and `created` frontmatter; typed notes (project,
  area, resource) also get `type` and `status`.
- Prefer wikilinks (`[[note-title]]`) over bare paths.

Folder layout (the PARA prompts read this; rename the folders to match your
vault):

- Inbox: `0-Inbox`
- Projects: `1-Projects`
- Areas: `2-Areas`
- Resources: `3-Resources`
- Archive: `4-Archive`
- Research notes: `3-Resources/research`
